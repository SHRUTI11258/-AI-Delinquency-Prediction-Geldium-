 AI-Powered Delinquency Prediction & Collections System
 Overview
This project was completed as part of the Tata iQ Virtual Experience Programme (via Forage). It covers the full analytics lifecycle — from data exploration to AI-powered autonomous system design — for a simulated financial services client (Geldium).

 What I Did

Task 1 — Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Analyzed a 500-customer dataset to identify gaps, anomalies, and key risk indicators for credit delinquency. Found that 16% of customers were delinquent, with high credit utilization and missed payments as the strongest predictors.

Task 2 — Predictive Modeling with GenAI
Built a Logistic Regression model for delinquency prediction using GenAI tools (ChatGPT/Gemini). Chose Logistic Regression over Neural Networks and Decision Trees for interpretability and regulatory compliance (ECOA, GDPR). Handled imbalanced data (84/16 split) and designed a fairness-aware evaluation strategy.

Task 3 — Business Report for Stakeholders
Translated model outputs into a stakeholder-ready business report for the Head of Collections. Identified high-risk segments (unemployed customers at 19.4%, young adults under 25 at 18.7%, high DTI customers at 18.3%) and recommended a 6-week pilot of a Hardship Support Initiative.

Task 4 — AI-Powered Collections System Design
Designed an autonomous AI system with agentic AI capabilities — including a data pipeline, decision engine, action layer, and continuous learning loop. Established Responsible AI guardrails for fairness, explainability, regulatory compliance (ECOA, GDPR, FCA, FCRA), and human-in-the-loop oversight.

 Tools & Skills
GenAI (ChatGPT, Gemini) | Logistic Regression | Predictive Modeling | Agentic AI | Data Storytelling | Ethical AI | Bias Detection | Regulatory Compliance | Business Reporting

 Dataset
500 customer records with 19 features including Credit Utilization, Missed Payments, DTI Ratio, Income, Credit Score, and Delinquent Account status.
